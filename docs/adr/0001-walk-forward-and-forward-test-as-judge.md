# Walk-forward plus a live Forward Test is the judge; the 2025–26 Holdout is spent

The first study used 2025-01..2026-09 as a Holdout and looked at it several times (the winner, then the VWAP-side addition). It can no longer tell a real Edge from a lucky one. From 2026-09-29 on, new Rule Sets are judged by Walk-Forward over 2016–2024 (tune on prior years, test on the next, roll forward) and must then pass a Forward Test of at least ~60 Sessions of paper trading with frozen parameters before real money. The Forward Test results are reviewed monthly and never used for tuning.

Considered: carving 2024 out as a new Holdout. It was rejected because it is small and was already seen as part of Validate.
