# NAS100 2003–2015 is a fresh Holdout, pre-registered before loading

ORB v1 lost money on ES (2016–24 Sharpe −0.46). That leaves two explanations: its Edge is specific to Nasdaq-like momentum instruments, or the NQ result is partly overfit. London Strategic Edge has NAS100/USD 1-minute index data from 2003, and none of it has been seen. On 2026-09-29, before downloading it, we fix the test:

- **Rule Set:** ORB v1 exactly as in `orb/families.py::ORB_V1` (5-min OR candle direction, body ≥ 0.05 × ATR14, with the gap, VWAP side, stop 0.10 × ATR14, 10R target, flat at 15:59). No parameter may change.
- **Data:** NAS100/USD 1m, Sessions 2003-01-01 … 2016-05-31 (the years before the NQ futures data). Index CFD volume stands in for futures volume in the VWAP.
- **Costs:** two cases, both reported. (a) proportional: 1 pt at NQ 12,000, i.e. about 0.83 bp of price per round trip; (b) a fixed 0.5 pt per round trip.
- **Pass:** under cost (a), Sharpe ≥ 0.5 and a positive Score in at least 60% of calendar years. Under cost (b), a positive total.
- It is looked at once. A pass supports "Nasdaq-specific Edge" and ORB v1 goes to the Forward Test. A fail means ORB v1 is treated as overfit, and the research restarts from the Walk-Forward.
